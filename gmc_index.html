The concept

Power at the fence. Heat for the town.

Grid power from the switchyard behind the fence runs the AI servers. Liquid cooling carries their heat away at a usable temperature, and a heat exchanger hands it over to the district-heating network of Visaginas. Energy that would otherwise be wasted warms the homes of the town.

Cool servers. Warm homes. Efficient by nature.

Why a data center

Europe needs its own AI capacity.

Sovereign AI

Compute is the new strategic infrastructure

Training and running AI models takes large, power-hungry data centers. Most of this capacity is still built in the United States. The EU wants enough of it on its own soil, under its own rules.

  • Data centers used about 415 TWh of electricity in 2024; the IEA expects that to roughly double by 2030.
  • The EU is funding AI Factories and AI Gigafactories (InvestAI, with a €20 bn fund for gigafactories).
  • The EU aims to at least triple its data center capacity within the next five to seven years.
  • The limit is power, not capital: few European sites can deliver 100 MW on a commercial timeline.

Heat for the town

Europe's AI, warming a town.

The servers produce heat all year. Where it goes depends on the season.

Winter. Liquid cooling carries the heat to a heat exchanger, and most of it flows into the district-heating network of Visaginas, warming homes.

Summer. The town needs heat mainly for hot water. The surplus is released through free cooling to the outside air; the nearby lake is a further option, subject to permits.

A second life

Where nuclear power once flowed, AI will run.

For a quarter of a century the Ignalina plant powered the country. Its reactors are gone, but the infrastructure around them, and the people, are still here.

  1. 1974Construction of the Ignalina nuclear power plant begins; the town of Visaginas is built for its workers.
  2. 1983Unit 1 starts: one of the most powerful reactors of its time.
  3. 1987Unit 2 starts. The plant becomes the backbone of the national grid.
  4. 2004Unit 1 is shut down, as agreed for EU accession.
  5. 2009Unit 2 is shut down; decommissioning begins. The grid connection stays.
  6. 2026GlobalMind Center requests a grid connection for a data center on the site.
  7. NextPower flows again: into AI compute, with the heat going back to the town.
